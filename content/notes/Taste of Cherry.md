> [!Summary] Synopsis
> A middle-aged Tehranian man, Mr. Badii is intent on killing himself and seeks someone to bury him after his demise. Driving around the city, the seemingly well-to-do Badii meets with numerous people, including a Muslim student, asking them to take on the job, but initially he has little luck. Eventually, Badii finds a man who is up for the task because he needs the money, but his new associate soon tries to talk him out of committing suicide.

## Personal Notes

I found it difficult to connect with this movie. While the story resonated with me in terms of dialectics of committing suicide, I didn't fully understand Kiarostami's choice of cinematography and overall scene set ups. I also did not appreciate the ending. I think it is one of those movies that need a rewatch.

- The film is incredibly simplistic. Often times, you don't even see the main actor in the same shot with whom he's talking.
