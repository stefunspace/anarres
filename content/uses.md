An overview of things I use on a daily basis.

- **Computer**: MacBook Air M2 with 16GB RAM. Probably the best laptop I've ever owned apart from a ThinkPad I used at my former company.
- **Mouse:** [Logitech MX Master 3](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mx-master-3s). One of the few items I would immediately buy again as soon as it breaks.
- **Keyboard:** [Keychron K2 Super Blue](https://www.keychron.com/products/keychron-k2-wireless-mechanical-keyboard?variant=31063869784153). This keyboard is affordable and a delight to type on. 
- **Headphones**: [Bose QuietComfort](https://www.bose.com/p/headphones/quietcomfort-acoustic-noise-cancelling-headphones/QC-HEADPHONEARN.html?dwvar_QC-HEADPHONEARN_color=PETAL%20PINK&quantity=1). One of the first purchases I made during the pandemic and the headphones are still going strong.
- **Browser**: Firefox with the following extensions (stars indicate must haves for an emancipatory internet experience):
	- Multi-Account Containers ★
		- an integral part of my browsing activity. It isolates web activity to the tab only, which means that cookies or other identifiable information are shared with other containers. I have containers for, if I ever use it, Google, Reddit, Amazon (I rarely buy anything anymore from Amazon), banking.
	- Adaptive Tab Bar Color
		- Changes the color of Firefox theme to match the website’s appearance.
	- AdNauseam ★
		- service that blocks ads and obfuscates browsing data. Must have!
	-  LibRedirect ★
		- redirects popular sites to alternative privacy-friendly frontends and backends
	- Obsidian Web Clipper
		- Saves and highlights web pages into Markdown and into my Obsidian vault
- **Terminal**: [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/).
- **Reading**: [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/)
- **Email**: [Protonmail](https://proton.me/mail). The gold standard of emails. In addition to their email service, I also use Drive and their VPN. I am a heavy user of email aliases, hence, I use [SimpleLogin](https://simplelogin.io/) together with Proton.
